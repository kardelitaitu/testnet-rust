# Basic Concepts of Blockchain

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/basic-concepts-of-blockchain](https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/basic-concepts-of-blockchain)

This chapter explains blockchain as a shared, immutable ledger used to track assets and transactions.

Main ideas:
- transactions are grouped into blocks
- blocks are chained together chronologically
- the chain becomes harder to alter as it grows

The doc frames blockchain as a transparent system that improves trust, traceability, and coordination.
