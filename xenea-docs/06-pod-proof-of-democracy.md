# PoD (Proof of Democracy)

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/overview/core-technology/pod-proof-of-democracy](https://docs.xenea.io/overview/core-technology/pod-proof-of-democracy)

PoD is Xenea’s consensus model using Rep nodes and Escrow nodes.

Main purpose:
- avoid PoS-style wealth concentration
- avoid PoW energy cost
- enable broad participation in validation

The mechanism uses randomly selected nodes and voting to validate transactions.
