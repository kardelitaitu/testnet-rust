# AML KYC Policy

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/other-documents/aml-kyc-policy](https://docs.xenea.io/other-documents/aml-kyc-policy)

This document is the compliance policy for the XENEA Wallet ecosystem.

Main points:
- anti-money-laundering controls
- terrorist-financing prevention
- non-custodial wallet positioning
- third-party service providers may apply their own KYC rules
