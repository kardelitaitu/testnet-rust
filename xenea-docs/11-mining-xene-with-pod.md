# Mining XENE with PoD

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/xene-tokenomics-1.0/mining-xene-with-pod](https://docs.xenea.io/xene-tokenomics-1.0/mining-xene-with-pod)

Mining rewards are distributed through PoD to consensus and storage participants.

Highlights:
- Escrow nodes, Rep nodes, and DACS nodes share block rewards
- rewards halve every two years
- no extra mining issuance beyond the documented cap

This page is the reward mechanics companion to the token supply page.
