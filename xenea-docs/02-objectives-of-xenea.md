# Objectives of Xenea

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/purpose-and-uniqueness](https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/purpose-and-uniqueness)

Xenea is designed to address several recurring blockchain problems:

- lower transaction cost
- long-term data persistence
- broader participation through representative nodes
- safer transfers through protocol-level escrow confirmation

The doc presents Xenea as a storage-first chain for assets that need durable ownership and availability.
