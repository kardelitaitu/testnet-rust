# Token Allocation and Supply

Source: [https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/token-allocation-and-supply](https://docs.xenea.io/whitepaper/token-allocation-and-supply)

XENE supply is split between genesis issuance and mining issuance.

Key figures from the doc:
- max supply: 18,328,109,640 XENE
- genesis allocation: 12,016,589,640 XENE
- mining allocation: 6,311,520,000 XENE

The doc also explains that mining rewards halve every two years.
